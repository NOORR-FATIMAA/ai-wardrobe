What it does

Get more out of the clothes you already have

Most of us wear a fraction of our wardrobe. Wardrobe AI helps you rediscover the rest.

Snap and it's tagged

Take a photo and the AI recognises the category, colours, material, formality and seasons. You review everything before it's saved.

An AI stylist for any occasion

Pick an occasion or just describe it, like "smart but comfortable for a long day at university". Outfits use only clothes you own, and can take the weather into account.

Chat about your style

"What goes with my black trousers?" "Make the second one more casual." Ask follow-ups and the stylist adjusts.

Plan and track

Plan looks for the next two weeks and tap "Wore it today". The stylist avoids repeating what you wore recently.

Learns your taste

Thumbs up the looks you love and thumbs down the rest. The stylist leans towards what you like and never suggests a disliked combination again.

Wardrobe insights

See your cost per wear, your hardest-working pieces, and the clothes you never reach for. Calculated on your device.

Smarter shopping

Find the real gaps in your wardrobe: versatile pieces that create the most new outfits with what you own, plus a list of things you don't need to buy.

Share a look

Send any outfit board as an image to a friend before you head out. It's created on your phone.

Clean cut-outs

In the iPhone and Android apps, backgrounds are removed on the device so your outfit boards look like a lookbook.

Privacy

Your wardrobe stays yours

  • ✓

    Private by default. Your photos are stored in a private folder only your account can access. Every record is locked to your account.

  • ✓

    AI only with your permission. AI features are off until you switch them on. When on, your clothing photos, wardrobe details and style preferences are sent to Google's Gemini API to tag clothes and suggest outfits.

  • ✓

    Location only when you ask. For weather-aware outfits, your approximate location (about 1 km) is used to fetch a forecast and is not stored.

  • ✓

    Delete everything, any time. Remove all your clothes, or delete your account together with every photo and record.

Details are in the privacy policy.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, during early access. There is a daily limit on AI requests per person so the service stays available for everyone.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. Outfits are built from clothes you own. You can switch off purchase suggestions completely and style only what's in your wardrobe.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. Open the web app and choose "Explore demo" to look around with a sample wardrobe. Nothing is saved or sent, and the demo stylist uses simple rules instead of AI.

Does it work for every style?

Yes. Menswear, womenswear, mixed and traditional clothing, from gym to wedding. Tell the stylist about your preferred styles, colours to avoid and how you like things to fit.

Is there a phone app?

The iPhone and Android apps are in development. The web app works in any modern browser on your phone or computer in the meantime.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. Photos are stored privately and shown to you through short-lived links. If you turn on AI features, the photos you analyse are processed by Google's Gemini API; see the privacy policy.

Ready to rediscover your wardrobe?

Start with five favourite pieces. It takes a couple of minutes.
