About

Fashion that starts in your own closet

Wardrobe AI is a personal styling app for people of every gender, body, culture and style, from gym to office to a Desi wedding. It’s built around one idea: the best outfit is usually already in your wardrobe.

It’s in early access and free to use, with a daily limit on AI requests so it stays available for everyone. iPhone and Android apps are in development; the web app works in any modern browser today.

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓

  • ✓

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, during early access. There is a daily limit on AI requests per person so the service stays available for everyone.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. Outfits are built from clothes you own. You can switch shopping ideas off and style only what’s in your wardrobe.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. Open the web app and choose “Explore demo” to look around with a sample wardrobe. Nothing is saved or sent, and the demo stylist uses simple rules instead of AI.

Does it work for every style?

Yes. Menswear, womenswear or both, and Western, Desi, Fusion, Modest or traditional dress, from gym to wedding. Tell the stylist your preferred styles, colours to avoid and how you like things to fit.

Is there a phone app?

The iPhone and Android apps are in development. The web app works in any modern browser on your phone or computer in the meantime.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. Photos are stored privately and shown to you through short-lived links. If you turn on AI features, the photos you analyse are processed by Google’s Gemini API; see the privacy policy.

Ready to love your wardrobe again?

Start with five favourite pieces. It takes a couple of minutes.
