What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Wardrobe: photos of your clothes and their details (name, category, colours, material, notes, and the optional price you paid).
  • Style profile: preferences you choose to give, such as styles, colours, fit, occasions, budget level, sizes and measurements.
  • Activity: saved and planned outfits, what you log as worn, your thumbs up / down on outfits, and your shopping list.
  • Usage counters: how many AI requests you made each day, to enforce fair-use limits.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell your data.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: storing your wardrobe, building outfits and insights, enforcing usage limits and keeping the service secure.

AI processing (only with your permission)

AI features are off until you switch them on in the app. When they are on, the data needed for a request (a clothing photo you analyse, your wardrobe details, style preferences, recent wears and outfit ratings, and the weather summary) is sent to the Google Gemini API to recognise clothes and suggest outfits. This includes the names and personal notes you gave your items. Your account email and display name are not sent.

Please avoid photos showing your face or anything private.

You can switch AI processing off at any time in your profile.

Location

Only when you ask for weather-aware outfits, your device's location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service to get a forecast. It is not stored.

Inspiration photos

When you tap See looks like this on an outfit, a short style phrase written by the stylist (for example "cream knit camel trousers minimal") is sent to Unsplash to find matching photos. It never includes your account details, item names or photos. The photos are then loaded directly from Unsplash, which, like any website, receives your device's IP address. Ideas on Pinterest opens Pinterest's own search page with the same phrase, under Pinterest's own privacy policy.

Where your data is stored

Your account, wardrobe data and photos are stored with Supabase (our database, authentication and file-storage provider) in [region of your Supabase project]. Photos are kept in a private storage area that only your account can access, and are shown to you through links that expire after an hour. Access rules in the database ensure each account can only read and change its own records.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while you have an account. In the app you can delete individual items, delete all your clothes, or delete your account, which permanently removes your account, photos and every record linked to it.
